# Data notice

Paper VII performs no endpoint fit to observational data. The files under
`data/derived/` are deterministic outputs of the public symbolic ledger audit
and the finite public-source eligibility audit. The latter records candidate
identifiers, public source links, six predeclared gates and per-candidate
verdicts; it does not redistribute the linked datasets. Earlier papers and the
Tau Core theory hub are dependency sources, not bundled private data.
The 14-candidate pair ledger and federated Gram-completion audit are likewise
derived metadata and deterministic identifiability calculations. They contain
source URLs and frozen hashes where available, but no external endpoint
payloads. Their non-identifiability verdict does not constitute a Tau score.
The frozen discovery protocol and the explicit external-payload hash boundary
are documented in `data/public_source_search_protocol.md`.
